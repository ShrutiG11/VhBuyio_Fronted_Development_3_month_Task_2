How to Play

  1. Everything is recorded. Each move or wait is one step of time.
  2. Press R to rewind. You jump back to the start, and your ghost replays your recording step by step, alongside you.
  3. Switches open doors of the same color while someone (you or your ghost) is standing on them.
  4. Reach the exit to unlock the next vault.
